Once a Brother, Always a Brother

Welcome to the Brotherhood 🎉

Your profile is approved. Here's every part of the site you now have access to — set up your profile first, then explore the rest in any order.

Start here

Two minutes of setup. Everything else on this page works better once these are done.

1

Fill in your profile

Click your name in the top-right corner → Brother Profile. Add a photo, your grad year, current city, occupation, and how brothers can reach you. Nearly every feature below reads from this: your pin on the map, your card in the directories, whether you turn up when a brother searches for someone in your field.

Open my profile →
2

Set up your account

Same menu → Account. Change your password, choose which emails you want, and switch on two-step verification if you'd like a code from your phone at sign-in. Your contact details are only ever shown to verified brothers, and you control which of them are visible.

Account settings →

Everything your membership unlocks

Twelve places to go. Click any card.

🎓

Active Brothers

The brothers on campus right now. Filter by pledge class, city or profession, and open any card for a full profile.

 🏛

Alumni Brothers

Three decades of brothers, same filters. Every card links to that brother's pledge class page, so you can see who he crossed with.

 🌳

Family Tree

Every brother since 1993, big to little, across all founding lines. Use Find a brother to jump straight to yourself.

 🗺

The Brotherhood Worldwide

A live map of where brothers are today, one pin per city. Add your current city to your profile and your pin appears.

 🖼

Brothers Gallery

A private photo feed only verified brothers can see. Post your own photos, file them under a section, like and comment — and edit or delete anything you posted.

 💬

Brothers Board

Five spaces: Introductions, Chapter Business, Advice, Social, and Opportunities — jobs and referrals brothers are offering or seeking. Attach a photo to any thread.

 🧭

Mentoring

Search alumni by skill, field or profession. Brothers who've opted in to mentoring come first — or post a request and let them find you.

 📅

Events & Philanthropy

Reunions, philanthropy, rush and homecoming on a month calendar. RSVP so the hosts know you're coming. Some events are members-only.

 🗳️

Polls

On the Board. Vote on chapter questions, or start your own poll — every brother can, and you can edit or close the ones you started.

 💡

Suggestion Box

Also on the Board. Send an idea or a problem to chapter leadership and see their reply when it comes back.

 🔔

Notifications

The bell in the header lights up for likes, comments, replies, mentor and connect requests, and chapter news. The full history lives here.

 👤

Profile & Account

Your details, privacy choices, email preferences, password and two-step verification — all in the menu under your name.

A few things worth knowing

  • Connect with a brother. Open anyone's profile and use Connect to send a short note. He gets a notification with your message and can reply by email.
  • Pledge class pages. Click any brother's pledge class in a directory to see everyone who crossed with him.
  • Dark mode. The moon icon in the header switches the whole site, and it remembers.
  • Chapter officers get an extra Officer Tools item in the account menu — events, committees, awards, gallery moderation and chapter email, depending on the seat.
  • Something look wrong? The contact form goes straight to the webmaster.

Once a brother, always a brother. Glad you're here. — ΖΒΞ
